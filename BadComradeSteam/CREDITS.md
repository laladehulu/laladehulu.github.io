# Bad Comrade — third-party assets and licences

This build uses only open-licensed, human-made assets for its icons, silhouettes and sound.
The world itself (soldiers, map, tanks, effects) is drawn at runtime from plain geometric shapes.

## Icons and silhouettes — Game-icons.net
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0) — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Source: https://game-icons.net (via the `@iconify-json/game-icons` package)

Icons used: brodie-helmet, alarm-clock, stick-grenade, handcuffs, cross-flare, wrench, eye-target,
notebook, rifle, winchester-rifle, m3-grease-gun, machine-gun, flamethrower, field-gun, rally-the-troops.

Authors: Lorc, Delapouite, and the Game-icons.net contributors (https://game-icons.net/about.html#authors).
Changes: recoloured, and some rotated or cropped to fit the interface.
