遼東LIAODONG · MING–QING WAR · 1619–1644

Command armies, fight in the ranks, or play the co-op campaigns of the war on the Liaodong frontier. Peer-to-peer multiplayer in the browser — no install. v104

RegularThe main game: ink-print art direction, all modes. RealismFilm-style lighting, smoke and dust, natural trees, with the experimental combat. F7 quality · F8 HDR. StylizedThe Realism game with clean painted graphics: flat toon light, crisp shadows, ink outlines, cel clouds. Same combat. F7 quality. Action combatThe Realism game with Dark-Souls-style melee: every attack, yours and theirs, is one of thirteen committed moves (cuts, chop, combo, thrust, lunge, charge, spin, leaping chop, pike flurry and sweep, shield bash), each read and answered by block, parry, dodge or shield. Battle experimentExperimental melee: physical blade arcs, two-attacker limit, no instant deaths.
