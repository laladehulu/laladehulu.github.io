    Chanyuan.io

    Hebei, 1004. Hundreds of commanders share one frontier. Song generals dig the water walls of the 塘泺; Khitan horsemen ride to break them. Any Song commander can buy a Liao warlord's protection with silk, and any warlord can betray the treaty.

    宋Song · dig the water wall

    • Leave your fields and you dig a line behind you. Return home to flood it: the loop becomes canal and everything inside becomes farmland that pays silk.
    • Canals cost horsemen speed and all their momentum. Fast riders dodge crossbow bolts; riders stuck in water don't.
    • A rider who crosses your unfinished line routs you. Space braces your spears: bolts hit half again as hard and charges break on you.
    • C builds a bed crossbow (80 silk) that hits fast riders hardest.

    遼Liao · ride and raid

    • Riding straight builds momentum. Speed means harder charges and dodged bolts. Sharp turns and water bleed it away.
    • Trample Song farmland for plunder. Cross a dig line before it closes to rout the digger.
    • Your bows fire on the move. Space reins in for a tight turn.
    • Retreat to an ordo camp to recover. Song fortresses shoot at you.

    盟The tribute treaty · 歲幣

    • A Song player presses T to offer 40% of their silk income to the nearest warlord, who accepts with Y.
    • Under the treaty, that warlord and every Liao ranked below him must leave you alone. Pay the strongest one you can find.
    • Tribute goes to the Liao court's score. One commander's peace can lose the year for the whole Song side.
    • A warlord can press B twice to betray every treaty at once. His charges hit double for 6 s, then he's branded Oathbreaker: Song bolts do ×1.8 and no Song can sign with him.
